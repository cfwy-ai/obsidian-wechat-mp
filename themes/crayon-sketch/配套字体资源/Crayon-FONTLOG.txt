Modification author: 长风无月 (Changfeng Wuyue)

The font adds sparse, tapered paper-colored openings inside existing glyph
outlines. The direction of each opening follows the local glyph stroke.
The exterior outline, Unicode character mapping, glyph order, horizontal
advances, and side bearings are preserved. Individual glyph components are
drawn from an immutable source before processing, avoiding double texture.

Coverage: all 7,053 original Unicode mappings; 7,055 glyphs.
Modified glyphs: 7,031.
Mean ink removed: 9.98 percent among modified glyphs.
Mean openings: 16.88 among modified glyphs.
No new system font installation is required: load the bundled font locally.

Recommended heading use: 28 px, Regular 400, orange #D97823.
Keep text paint disabled. Do not apply synthetic bold; it fills small gaps.
This is a monochrome outline font: the openings are truly transparent, so
they automatically reveal the paper background. It does not add color
variation or reproduce a particular image's lettering pixel for pixel.

Validation: all glyphs fully decompiled; source cmap, glyph order, and hmtx
match; representative Chinese headings render without missing glyphs.
A Chromium specimen was verified with computed 28 px size and loaded font.
